FAQ

Answers to common questions to help you understand how we work.

Do you guarantee a higher settlement?

No. We provide independent claim review and support services. Outcomes depend on policy terms, evidence, and insurer decisions.

What documents do you need?

Usually: policy schedule, insurer letters/emails, claim reference, photos, quotes/valuations, and any reports. We’ll send a checklist.

How quickly can you start?

We begin from claim pre-review. Once you provide documents (and any required authority), we can commence promptly.

Can you help with motor total loss disputes?

Yes—where appropriate, we focus on evidence such as market comparisons, vehicle condition, kilometres, and options.

Do you communicate directly with my insurer?

Yes, if you authorise us. Otherwise, we can provide templates and guidance for you to use.

What does the process look like once I engage you?

Once engaged, we review your documents, outline the approach, then support correspondence, documentation, follow-ups, and escalation where appropriate.

Do you provide financial or legal advice?

No. Claimanage does not provide financial, legal, or insurance advice. We provide independent claim review, documentation support, and claim process assistance only.
